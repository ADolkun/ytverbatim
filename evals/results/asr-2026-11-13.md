## Owner judging

The owner judged without the terminal loop for most spans. The spans were sorted by kind before the key was opened,
and each kind got one owner policy; only real content differences were judged one by one, with each clip's YouTube
timestamp at hand.

| kind | spans | owner policy | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| written form only (digits against Chinese numerals, spacing) | 15 | both fine | — |
| fillers, repeats and false starts | 72 | both fine | w-prod's reading was the shorter in 59, m-4bit's in 13 |
| small words that leave the meaning unchanged | 21 | both fine | — |
| same sound, different characters or Latin case | 22 | the correctly written reading wins | m-4bit 19, w-prod 3 |
| content differences | 20 | judged one by one | m-4bit 10, w-prod 6, both wrong 4 |

m-4bit keeps the fillers and repeats that w-prod drops. Of its 12 English wins, 7 come from spelling or case, 5 of them
capitalization alone; in a live run the production punctuation step restored the capitals of such acronyms in
w-prod's output, so part of that edge does not reach a finished transcript.
